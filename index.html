What it does

Care, without the guesswork.

Point, and it knows the plant

Identify a species from a photo. The model runs on your iPhone, so photos never leave it.

Schedules that read the sky

Watering shifts with local humidity and rain, and outdoor plants get a heads-up before a downpour.

Health and soil checks

Snap a leaf or the soil to see whether a plant is thirsty, drowning or just fine.

Done from the Home Screen

Widgets for the Home Screen and Lock Screen show what’s due. Check it off without opening the app.

Tell Siri you watered

“I watered the Fiddle Fig in Terrarium.” Logged, and the next reminder moves.

Reminders that respect quiet hours

A nudge when care is due, never at 2 a.m. Seasons and hemisphere are handled for you.

Privacy

Your garden stays yours.

Everything you add lives on your device. There is no server on the other end collecting it.

Read the privacy policy →

  • No account. Open it and start. There’s nothing to sign in to.
  • No tracking. No ads, analytics or third-party SDKs of any kind.
  • On-device. Identification, health and soil checks all run locally.
